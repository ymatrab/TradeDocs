## Change rules

Create an ADR from [docs/adr/0000-template.md](docs/adr/0000-template.md) for consequential architecture, provider, schema/version or requirement interpretation. State alternatives, security/privacy/legal impacts, migration/rollback and acceptance evidence. Scope deviations require actual recorded product approval and rationale/impact; an engineering interpretation is not permission to omit scope. Record supersession rather than rewriting historical approvals. Do not put credentials or private customer data in decision records.
