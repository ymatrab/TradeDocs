Every tool CTA follows `primaryAction(isDatabaseConfigured())`, so no page offers sign-up while accounts are closed. Rule pages are generated from `src/lib/trade/incoterms.ts` (including the optional `search` title, heading and lead, and `INCOTERMS_CHART` for the hub chart); guides from `src/lib/content/guides/` and posts from `src/lib/content/posts/`, one file per article (`<slug>.ts`) listed in that folder's `index.ts`, so writers can work in parallel without touching the same lines. Article-specific sources go in `src/lib/content/sources/<slug>.ts` and merge into the registry; a repeated id stops the build. An article's optional `related` paths choose its foot links (at most six; otherwise the newest of the same kind). FAQ data is exported so FAQPage JSON-LD matches visible text; guides also carry Article and BreadcrumbList JSON-LD with a team byline. The tool list, sitemap, llms.txt and related-tools block all read `PUBLIC_TOOLS` and `GUIDES`, and the home "free tools" count is `PUBLIC_TOOLS.length`. The delivery note, container loading and unit converter pages (2026-10-07) keep their FAQ arrays in `src/lib/tools/faq.ts`, outside the content-owned folder; the help centre does not search them until they move into `src/lib/content/faq.ts`.

Guides and blog posts share one data shape (`src/lib/content/article.ts`) and one renderer (`ArticleView`). Every article carries, in this order: hero with team byline and published / updated / last-reviewed dates, credited cover, **Short answer** (40–60 words, tested) with the in-context tool button, **Key facts** (quotable one-line statements naming the entity: ICC, Incoterms® 2020, CBP 19 CFR 141.86, ITA), **Terms used** (definitions), question-style H2 sections with step lists and comparison tables, a mid-article tool callout, "Tools for this", the visible FAQ (same array as FAQPage), the not-advice note, sources, the closing ToolCta with a plain `/pricing` link, and related articles. `tests/unit/posts.test.ts` enforces the structure for posts and guides. Posts: 1,200–1,800 visible words; guides: 900–1,500.

Keyword split to avoid cannibalisation: "what is a proforma invoice" (4,400) stays with `/tools/proforma-invoice-generator`, which answers it; the proforma post targets "example" and "meaning". "Commercial invoice template" stays with the invoice generator; the post targets requirements and how to fill one out. The export documents checklist mentions proof of origin only as a document others issue and says TradeDocs does not prepare it (D-008); the phrase "certificate of origin" stays off public articles (tested).

Not written: "Incodocs alternatives". The plan measured 0 US searches and defers it; it needs a sales reason and claims dated from Incodocs' live pages.

The landed cost calculator has no tariff or tax data: every rate and both valuation bases are entered by the visitor, arithmetic is exact decimal in `src/lib/trade/landed-cost.ts` (unit-tested), and the page lists its assumptions with a not-advice note.

## Source registry

All cited sources live in [`src/lib/trade/sources.ts`](src/lib/trade/sources.ts) (authority, title, URL, jurisdiction, what it supports, retrieval date, reviewer) and render through `SourcesBlock`. Each URL was opened on 2026-10-05 and checked against its claim. The eCFR pages refuse automated readers, so 19 CFR 141.85 and 141.86 were checked through the eCFR versioner API; the Maersk FCL vs LCL article was read from its HTML because the fetch tool rejected its headers.

Added 2026-10-06 (blog round): ITA Common Export Documents (`trade-gov-export-documents`), cited for the document list, the bill of lading as carrier contract and the EEI $2,500-per-Schedule-B threshold. Re-read the same day for the posts: ITA commercial invoice and packing list pages, and 19 CFR 141.86 through the eCFR versioner API (paragraph (d) English-language and (e) per-package contents); their retrieval dates stay 2026-10-05 because the claims they were first cited for are unchanged.

Added 2026-10-05: ITA pro forma invoice, commercial invoice and packing list pages; 19 CFR 141.85; NIST SP 811 Appendix B.9 (ft³ factor); Maersk FCL vs LCL; WTO customs valuation technical information.

No primary source found (2026-10-05): the 333 kg/m³ European road groupage ratio and the 1 t/m³ sea LCL W/M ratio (`VOLUMETRIC_RULES` in `src/lib/trade/calculations.ts`). Carriers set both in their own tariffs, and DHL's chargeable-weight explainer, which mentions a 1:3 road ratio, could not be opened by the fetch tools, so neither has a source record. Every surface labels them "a common industry convention; check your carrier's / forwarder's tariff" (calculator hints, chargeable weight FAQ, LCL vs FCL guide).

Container capacities (`CONTAINERS`) now match Maersk's dry container sheet, re-read 2026-10-05: 20' standard 33 m³ / 28,200 kg, 40' standard 67 m³ / 28,800 kg, 40' high cube 76 m³ / 28,620 kg, 45' high cube 85 m³ / 27,600 kg (steel; one carrier's fleet, labelled typical). A unit test pins the four pairs.
