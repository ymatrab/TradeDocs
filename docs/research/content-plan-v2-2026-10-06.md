## How the 90 pages were chosen

1. **Demand first.** Each page has a primary keyword with measured volume, or a named conversion role when volume is low (marked "conversion role").
2. **One intent, one URL.** Queries that a tool page or an Incoterms rule page already answers stay there (see "Routed to existing pages"). Posts never target a rule's meaning ("fob meaning", "ddp meaning"); the rule pages own those.
3. **Product fit.** Every page leads to one of the seven public tools. Pages with no document or tool tie were excluded even with large volume.
4. **Posts vs guides.** A **guide** is a reference explainer for a definition or a comparison of concepts ("what is a bill of lading", "gross vs net weight"): 900–1,500 words, evergreen. A **post** is a task, scenario or rule comparison ("commercial invoice for samples", "EXW vs FOB"): 1,200–1,800 words. Both use the same shape and tests.
5. **No per-variant pages without demand.** Per-country commercial invoice pages measured 10 a month (Australia, Mexico, China, India, Japan, UK), so only Canada (480 + 480) gets its own post; the UK and EU share one. Carrier variants share one post.

Counts: 60 posts = 5 published + 55 planned. 30 guides = 3 published + 27 planned. Launch batch 1 = 20 new posts + 10 new guides.

- **Certificate of origin gate (D-002):** "certificate of origin" (4,400), "rules of origin" (260), USMCA/CUSMA certification. Public articles must not use the phrase while the gate holds (tested). Plan these when the gate lifts.
- **Regulated, no tool:** dangerous goods declaration (480), dangerous goods note (UK 260).
