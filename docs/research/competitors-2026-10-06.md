# Competitor analysis — trade documents for small exporters

Prepared by the marketing agent, 2026-10-06 (D-020). Every figure below was read from the
competitor's own live page on **2026-10-06** with WebFetch (a text extraction of the page, not a
browser render). Anything the page did not state is marked **not verified**. Prices are as shown
on the page, before any tax; none was checked in a logged-in account or at checkout. Re-check
before quoting any figure in public copy, and never name these products on the homepage
(rules/product.md).

Why these six: IncoDocs is the domain that dominates our SERPs (48 hits across the 2026-10-05
DataForSEO pulls in `docs/research/dataforseo-2026-10-05/`, with its own relevant-pages file).
ovrseas sells the same "one master file, every document" idea at small-exporter prices. Shipping
Solutions is the long-standing US desktop incumbent. Zoho Invoice, Refrens and
Invoice-Generator.com are what a small exporter finds when they search for a free invoice tool
instead of trade software.

Not covered: CargoWise and other forwarder TMS suites (enterprise sales, no public price list, a
different buyer), Freightos (its freight-tools URL returned 404 on 2026-10-06; not verified),
TradeGecko (now QuickBooks Commerce; not trade documents).

## 2. What the market says

1. **The market meters documents.** IncoDocs (10/30/100/300) and ovrseas (30/100/300) both cap
   documents per month and seats per plan. Paid entry sits at $20–27 a month for one user.
2. **Seats are the second meter.** 1 user at entry, 2–3 mid, 5 at the top before "contact
   sales".
3. **20% off yearly is the norm** (IncoDocs, ovrseas).
4. **14-day trials, no card**, are standard for the paid trade tools.
5. **Free generic tools win the "free" intent** but save nothing.

## 3. Where TradeDocs is already better (facts from our code)

- **One record, every document.** Commercial invoice, proforma, packing list and delivery note
  come from one shipment revision (`src/lib/billing/plans.ts` FEATURES), so totals, parties and
  weights agree. Competitors share a master file; whether their documents stay consistent after
  an edit is not verified.
- **Immutable revisions with stale marking.** Documents are locked to the revision they came
  from and marked stale when the shipment changes. IncoDocs states "version control"; ovrseas
  and the free tools state nothing comparable.
- **Free generators with no account and nothing stored**, plus CBM, dimensional weight, landed
  cost and an Incoterms 2020 reference. IncoDocs' templates need an account.
- **No meter today.** No cap on shipments, documents or members, while competitors charge from
  the 11th (IncoDocs) or 31st (ovrseas) document a month.
- **ZIP of the whole set with a checksum manifest** (up to 60 documents). Not seen on any
  competitor page.
- **Incoterms content.** Guides, blog posts and the reference page, with primary sources.
